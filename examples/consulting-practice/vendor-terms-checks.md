# Example Advisory: vendor terms checks

*Fictional worked example of the [vendor terms check](../../kit/vendor-terms-check.md). The vendors are described generically; the answers show the kind of evidence to record, not any real vendor's terms. Licensed CC-BY-4.0.*

## Office-suite assistant, business plan (CP-02)

Checked by the office manager, 2026-10-20.

| Question | Answer | Where it says so |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Training on our inputs or outputs? | No, for business plans | Product-specific terms for the business plan, "AI features" section |
| 2. How long are prompts and outputs kept? | In our own tenant, under our existing retention settings | Same section |
| 3. DPA covering this plan? | Yes, the vendor's standard DPA applies | Order confirmation lists the DPA |
| 4. Who else receives our data? | The vendor's model provider, listed as a subprocessor | Subprocessor list, current version |
| 5. Where is it processed? | Our tenant's region | Admin console, data location page |

## Video-call notetaker, business plan (CP-03)

Checked by the office manager, 2026-10-20.

| Question | Answer | Where it says so |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Training on our inputs or outputs? | No, for business workspaces, including de-identified or aggregated recordings; the terms state it applies to all business customers without an opt-out step | Business terms, "Customer data" section |
| 2. How long are prompts and outputs kept? | Our admin sets retention; we set 90 days | Business terms, same section; admin console retention page |
| 3. DPA covering this plan? | Yes, incorporated into the business terms | Business terms, "Data processing" clause |
| 4. Who else receives our data? | Due 2026-11-19 | — |
| 5. Where is it processed? | Due 2026-11-19 | — |

Admin settings checked the same day: automatic sharing of summaries is off, auto-join is limited to meetings the user hosts, and speaker recognition uses names from the invite, not voiceprints.

## Writing assistant (CP-04)

Checked by the office manager, 2026-10-20.

| Question | Answer | Where it says so |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Training on our inputs or outputs? | Not stated for the plan in use; the free plan allows it | We could not confirm which plan the three consultants are on |
| 2. How long are prompts and outputs kept? | Not stated | — |
| 3. DPA covering this plan? | Only on the business plan | Vendor's pricing page (a lead, not evidence) |
| 4. Who else receives our data? | Not checked | — |
| 5. Where is it processed? | Not checked | — |

**Result:** retention and training, and contract coverage, both rated Severe and marked unknown. Action: the founder asks the three consultants which plan they are on; if it is a free plan, move them to the business plan and check its terms.
