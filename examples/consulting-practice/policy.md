# Example Advisory: AI use policy

*Fictional worked example of the Level 1 sections of the [policy template](../../kit/policy.md). Licensed CC-BY-4.0.*

**Owner:** Founder **Approved by:** Founder **Effective:** 2026-10-22 **Next review:** 2027-10-22

## 1. Why this policy exists

We use AI tools to work faster. Our clients trust us with their strategy and, sometimes, their customers' information. This policy says which AI tools we may use, what may go into them, and who decides.

## 2. Who it covers

All five of us and any contractor working on client matters, on any device, including AI features inside software we already use.

## 3. The owner

The founder owns this policy and decides. When the founder is away, the senior consultant decides. If you are unsure, ask before you use it.

## 4. Approved and banned tools

| Tool and plan | Allowed for | Conditions | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Office-suite assistant, our business plan | Internal and client work | Web search off; the assistant drafts but sends nothing; review every client-facing draft before sending | Allow with conditions |
| Chat assistant, our business plan | Internal and client work | Web browsing, connectors and link previews stay off; no client customer lists | Allow with conditions |
| Video-call notetaker, our business plan | Internal and client calls | Automatic sharing stays off; announce recording and ask for consent at the start; host reviews notes before sharing | Allow with conditions |
| Chat assistant, personal accounts | Nothing involving firm or client information | — | Banned |
| Writing assistant | Nothing involving client information, until its plan is confirmed | — | Banned |

Anything not on this list needs the founder's approval before you use it for work.

## 5. What information may go into AI tools

| Information | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Public information | Any approved tool |
| Our internal business information | Approved tools on a business plan |
| Client information, including financial statements and anything else under an NDA | Only the tools whose "Allowed for" in section 4 includes client work, as configured there, on business terms that bar the vendor from training on it. Redact account numbers from statements first |
| Client customer lists and personal data | Not in any AI tool without the client's written agreement |
| Regulated information (health, payment or account numbers, government IDs, information about children) | Not in any AI tool |

Two clients' contracts restrict AI use on their material; their matters use no AI tools.

## 6. Using what AI produces

A consultant reviews anything AI produced before it goes to a client. You are responsible for what you send. We tell clients how we use AI using the wording in our [client disclosure](client-disclosure.md).

## 7. Asking first and exceptions

Tools rated High are "ask first": the founder decides once for that tool and purpose, in writing, and records the decision and its conditions in the inventory. If a condition stops holding, such as a setting being switched back on, the tool goes back to the founder. Exceptions are in writing, with the date, conditions and a review date, and are recorded in the inventory.

## 8. When something goes wrong

Stop, and follow the [incident card](incident-card.md). Tell the founder the same day.

## 9. Review

The founder reviews this policy every October. Each tool is reviewed on the date in its inventory row: a year for Low and Medium, six months for High, three months for a Severe exception. Review sooner whenever someone wants a new AI tool or a vendor changes its terms.
