# Example Advisory: AI incident card

*Fictional worked example of the [incident card](../../kit/incident-card.md). Licensed CC-BY-4.0.*

## Something went wrong with an AI tool

Client information went into a tool it shouldn't have; the notetaker shared something nobody approved; someone acted on wrong AI output; a vendor told us about a breach.

## Do this now

1. **Stop** using the tool for the affected work.
2. **Contain:**

| Tool | How to switch it off | Who can do it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office-suite assistant | Admin console, AI features, turn off for the affected user | Office manager |
| Video-call notetaker | Admin settings, disable the notetaker for the account; delete the meeting's notes | Office manager |
| Writing assistant | Uninstall the browser extension; sign out | The person using it |

3. **Tell the founder today:** [phone], [email]. Backup: senior consultant, [phone].
4. **Write down** what happened, when, what client information was involved, and which client. Keep screenshots.
5. **Don't delete anything else, and don't contact the client** until the founder has decided what to say.

## The founder then decides

Whether the client must be told (check their contract for notice terms; two clients require notice within 48 hours), whether to ask the vendor to delete data, whether to call the insurer, and what changes in the policy or inventory.
