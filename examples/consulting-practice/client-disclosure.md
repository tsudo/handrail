# Example Advisory: client disclosure

*Fictional worked example of the [client disclosure language](../../kit/client-disclosure.md). Licensed CC-BY-4.0.*

## Added to the engagement letter

> We use AI tools to help with research, drafting and meeting notes. We use only tools on business terms that do not allow the provider to train on your information, and a member of our team reviews everything we deliver to you. We do not put your customer lists or personal data into AI tools without your written agreement. If you would prefer we did not use AI tools on your work, tell us and we will agree how to proceed.

## Said at the start of a recorded call

> We use a notetaker on our calls. It records and transcribes, and the notes stay in our account for 90 days. Is everyone comfortable with that? We can turn it off.
