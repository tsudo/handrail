# Review calendar (Level 2)

*How often each approved AI use case is reviewed, and what starts a review early. The program owner keeps the dates in the AI register.*

## By tier

| Tier | Decision target (working days from a complete request) | Reviewed | Exceptions reviewed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | 2 | Yearly | — |
| Medium | 5 | Every six months | — |
| High | 15 | Quarterly | — |
| Severe | 30 | Monthly | Monthly, and every exception has an end date |

## Reviews that start early

Any of these starts a review of the affected use case within two weeks:

- The vendor changes its terms, plan, model or data handling.
- The vendor adds AI to a product we already use, or switches an AI feature on by default.
- A new connector, integration or MCP server is added.
- The use case expands to new data, new users or a new purpose.
- An incident, near miss or complaint involves the tool.
- The vendor is acquired or has a security incident.
- A law or a client contract changes what is allowed.

## Calendar

| Month | What is due |
| --- | --- |
| Every month | Severe exceptions; overdue findings |
| Every quarter | High-tier use cases; committee report |
| Every six months | Medium-tier use cases |
| Every year | Low-tier use cases; the AI policy; the roles sheet; staff briefing refresher |
