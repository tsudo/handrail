# Staff briefing: using AI at [organization name] (Level 1 recommended, Level 2 required)

*A one-page briefing to share with everyone and to cover when someone joins. Keep a record of who has read it (Level 2).*

## The short version

- Use only the AI tools on our approved list, on the plan we approved.
- Never put client or customer information into a personal or free AI account.
- Regulated information (health, payment, government ID numbers, anything about children) does not go into AI tools without written approval.
- You review anything AI produces before it goes to a client or customer, or is used to decide something about a person.
- Want a new tool, or to use an approved one in a new way? Ask [owner or intake form] first.
- Something went wrong? Stop, and tell [name] the same day.

## Why

Our clients trust us with their information, and many of our contracts say how we may use it. AI tools can keep what you type, use it to train their models, or send it to other companies. The approved list is the set we have checked.

## Watch for

- AI features that appear in software we already use. Ask before switching one on.
- Tools that read web pages, emails or files from outside and can also send things. They can be tricked by what they read.
- Output that sounds confident and is wrong. Check facts, numbers and quotes.
