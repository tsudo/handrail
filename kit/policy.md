# AI use policy

*Template. Replace the bracketed text. Fill the Level 1 sections; add the Level 2 sections when you reach Level 2. Delete these italic notes when you are done.*

**[Organization name]: AI use policy**
**Owner:** [name] **Approved by:** [name] **Effective:** [date] **Next review:** [date, at most a year away]

## 1. Why this policy exists (Level 1)

We use AI tools to work faster. Our clients and customers trust us with their information, and we owe them care with it. This policy says which AI tools we may use, what information may go into them, and who decides.

## 2. Who it covers (Level 1)

Everyone who works for or with [organization name], on any device, whenever the work involves our information or our clients' information. It covers AI features inside software we already use, not only standalone AI tools.

## 3. The owner (Level 1)

[Name] owns this policy and makes the decisions in it. When [name] is away, [backup name] decides. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask the owner before you use it, not after.

## 4. Approved and banned tools (Level 1)

*Copy each tool's decision from the inventory. A tool used two ways can appear twice.*

| Tool and plan | Allowed for | Conditions | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [e.g. an office-suite assistant, business plan] | [Internal and client work] | [Web search off; review every client-facing draft before it is sent] | Allow with conditions |
| [e.g. a chat assistant, personal accounts] | [Nothing involving client or company information] | — | Banned |
| [Tool] | [Use] | [Conditions] | [Allow / Allow with conditions / Banned / Exception] |

*"Allowed for" says which work the tool may be used on, such as "internal only" or "internal and client work". Take it from the inventory's "Used for" column. Section 5 refers to it.*

Anything not on this list needs the owner's approval before you use it for work.

## 5. What information may go into AI tools (Level 1)

| Information | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Public information | Any approved tool |
| Our internal business information | Approved tools on a business plan |
| Client or customer information, including financial statements and anything else under an NDA | Only tools whose "Allowed for" in section 4 includes client work, on business terms that bar the vendor from training on it. Never personal or free accounts |
| Regulated information (health data, payment card or financial account numbers, government ID numbers, voiceprints, information about children) | Not in any AI tool unless the owner grants an exception, or the tool meets every condition for regulated data the firm already handles (see the Level 1 start page). Redact account and card numbers from statements before upload; the statement is then client information |

*If any client contract restricts AI use on their material, add the client and the restriction here.*

## 6. Using what AI produces (Level 1)

- A person reviews anything AI produced before it goes to a client or customer, or is used to make a decision about someone.
- You are responsible for work you send, whoever or whatever drafted it.
- [If AI output reaches clients:] We tell clients how we use AI, using the wording in our [client disclosure](client-disclosure.md).

## 7. Asking first and exceptions (Level 1)

"Ask first" applies to tools rated High: the owner decides once, in writing, for that tool and purpose, and records the decision (Allow with conditions, or Banned) and any conditions in the inventory. After that, everyday use under those conditions needs no further asking. If a condition stops holding, such as a setting being switched back on, the tool goes back to the owner.

An exception lets someone use a tool or information the policy would not otherwise allow. It is in writing, names who granted it, the date, the conditions and a review date, and is recorded in the inventory.

## 8. When something goes wrong (Level 1)

If you think information went somewhere it shouldn't, or an AI tool did something unexpected, stop using it and follow the [incident card](incident-card.md). Tell the owner the same day.

## 9. Review (Level 1)

*At Level 2, section 10 and the [review calendar](review-calendar.md) replace the review dates below.*

The owner reviews this policy at least once a year. Each tool is reviewed on the date in its inventory row: a year for Low and Medium, six months for High, three months for a Severe exception. Review sooner when we start using a new kind of AI tool or a vendor changes its terms.

## 10. Tiers and decisions (Level 2)

Every AI use case is assessed on the Three Surfaces model and given a tier. The tier decides who approves it, how fast, and how often it is reviewed.

| Tier | Who decides | Who accepts the remaining risk | Decision within | Reviewed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | [Security reviewer] | [Business owner of the use case] | 2 working days | Yearly |
| Medium | [Department lead with security review] | [Business owner of the use case] | 5 working days | Every six months |
| High | [AI governance committee] | [Business unit head] | 15 working days | Quarterly |
| Severe | Exception only: [CISO and the committee, with an executive sponsor] | [A named executive] | 30 working days | Monthly |
| Severe, regulated data on the designed path | [The committee with the CISO] | [Business unit head] | 30 working days | Quarterly |

*The designed path applies only when the organization runs a program for that kind of regulated data (listed in its profile), data sensitivity is the only Severe rating, and every required treatment is Identified and confirmed in place by someone other than the requester. Everything else Severe is exception only.*

## 11. Roles (Level 2)

Roles and responsibilities are set out in [roles](roles.md). The committee, if there is one, works to the [governance charter](charter.md).

## 12. Requests and records (Level 2)

New AI tools and new uses of existing tools start with the [intake form](intake-form.md). Every assessed use case has an [assessment record](assessment-record.md) and a row in the AI register. An AI system impact assessment is completed when any People question is rated High or above, or when the lethal trifecta applies.
