# AI governance charter (Level 2)

*Template for organizations with an AI governance committee or review board. If decisions at High sit with one person instead, keep sections 1, 4 and 6 and name that person.*

## 1. Purpose

The [committee name] decides on High-tier AI use cases, recommends Severe exceptions, and keeps the AI policy working. It exists so that higher-risk AI use gets a considered, recorded decision within a set time, and lower-risk use is not held up.

## 2. Members

| Executive sponsor (for Severe exceptions) | |

Quorum: [three members, including the chair or a delegate and security].

## 3. Meetings

[Every two weeks], or sooner when a High request would otherwise miss its 15-working-day decision target. Decisions can be taken asynchronously when every member has responded.

## 4. What the committee decides

- High-tier use cases: approve, approve with conditions, hold, or decline.
- Severe-tier requests: recommend or decline an exception. The executive sponsor grants it.
- Changes to the policy, the ceilings and the decision targets.

Low and Medium decisions stay with the people the policy names.

## 5. How a decision is recorded

In section 2 of the assessment record: the decision, conditions, who decided, the date, who accepts the remaining risk, any exception reference and the next review date. The program owner updates the AI register.

## 6. Speed and escalation

| Situation | Response |
| --- | --- |
| An AI tool is causing harm now | Anyone named on the roles sheet can disable it; tell the chair within the hour |
| A High request would miss its decision target | The chair decides alone and reports at the next meeting |
| Unapproved AI tools are found in use | The program owner records them in the register within a week; the owner of the use case has 30 days to bring them through intake or stop |

## 7. Reporting

Each quarter the chair reports to [leadership]: requests received and decided, decision times against target, open exceptions, findings open past their due date, and incidents.
