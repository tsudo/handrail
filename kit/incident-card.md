# AI incident card (Level 1)

*Print this, fill it in, and keep it where people will find it.*

## Something went wrong with an AI tool

Examples: client or customer information went into a tool it shouldn't have; an AI tool sent, posted or changed something nobody approved; someone acted on AI output that turned out to be wrong; a vendor told us about a breach.

## Do this now

1. **Stop.** Stop using the tool for the affected work.
2. **Contain.** If it is still running, switch it off or remove its access. How, for each tool:

| Tool | How to switch it off or disconnect it | Who can do it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | | |
| | | |

*Where the switch usually is:*

| Kind of tool | Usually |
| --- | --- |
| Business plan of a chat or writing assistant | Remove the person from the workspace, or turn the feature off in the admin console |
| AI inside your office suite or email | The admin center's AI or "copilot" settings, per user or for everyone |
| Meeting notetaker | Disconnect its calendar access, turn off auto-join and auto-share, remove the bot from the meeting |
| Connector or integration | Revoke the app's access in the connected system's settings (email, files, CRM) |
| Personal account | You cannot switch it off: tell the person to stop and change any shared password |

3. **Tell the owner today.** [Name], [phone], [email]. Backup: [name], [phone].
4. **Write down** what happened, when, what information was involved, and who was affected. Keep screenshots.
5. **Keep the evidence.** Screenshot or export before you change anything. Deleting a copy that went somewhere it shouldn't, such as an over-shared meeting summary, is containment: record what you deleted and when. Don't contact the client or vendor until the owner has decided what to say.

## The owner then decides

- Whether a client or customer needs to be told, and what to tell them. Check the client's contract for notice requirements.
- Whether to contact the vendor (for example, to ask for deletion).
- Whether insurance or legal advice is needed.
- What changes in the [policy](policy.md) or the [inventory](inventory.md) so it doesn't happen again.
